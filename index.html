Rekindling Targets

Messaging on the show floor

"If someone were to Google you, what would they find?"

One shared narrative, carried across every conversation: we help companies actively build a digital presence and reputation — a layered, cumulative mosaic that proves who they are and why they matter. If they don't shape that narrative, it gets shaped for them by algorithms, competitors, AI summaries, and search results that flatten nuance. This only works if we're consistent.

Operating rhythm

  • Huddles: two a day — end of morning session (~1pm) and end of day (~5pm). Share breakthroughs, reprioritize booths, flag "drop everything" moments.
  • Yearbook: ~100 photos across the team (~20 each). Priority on buyer shots, strong booth visuals, candid conversation moments.
  • Shop Talks: outside videographer confirmed — coordinate subject list and run-of-show on the floor.
  • Anchored meals: Reid → BelGioioso · Melissa → Venus · Whit → F&S · Jordan → Mama's Creations & Taylor Farms.
  • Questions we're asking: Where is IDDBA heading? What feels different in the market? Wins this past year? Where's the pressure? Where can stronger storytelling and visibility help?
